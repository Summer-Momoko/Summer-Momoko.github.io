<p>After a decade in private equity and software M&A across Asia, I moved to the Bay Area to begin my MBA at Berkeley Haas. I wanted to immerse myself in the world's most dynamic AI and enterprise software ecosystem, not just by studying it, but by building alongside founders, operators, and investors.</p>

<p>I am now Chief of Staff at Tumeryk, an AI security and governance platform that helps enterprises adopt generative AI safely and responsibly. I work with the team on go-to-market strategy, competitive intelligence, strategic partnerships, and strategic finance. It has been a shift from evaluating software companies as an investor to helping build one from the inside.</p>

<p>What I'm most curious about now is how AI is reshaping enterprise software, and what security, trust, and governance will mean as AI becomes embedded in the way companies operate.</p>
